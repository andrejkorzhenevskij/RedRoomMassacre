## Core pillars

1. Combat feel
The game must feel brutal, slow, physical and lethal.
Every hit should have weight.
The player should fear mistakes.

2. Camera mechanic
The handheld VHS camera is the unique identity of the game.
It changes how the player approaches violence.

3. Damage and gore feedback
Body damage, wounds and dismemberment support the feeling of physical violence.

The camera is not a replacement for combat.
It is a system built on top of a strong combat foundation.

## Combat direction

Reference feeling:
slow, lethal, physical close combat.

Avoid:
arcade combat, damage sponge enemies, fast combo action.

The player should feel like every confrontation is dangerous.
